# Mode: Customer Proof Sprint

## Intake
Required: source pack; audience; permission/approval status; outcome metric with denominator and period or a clearly qualitative result. Optional: customer quotes with speaker/locator, process description, limitations, implementation context, requested channels. If permission is missing, create an internal anonymized draft and a release gap list.

## Procedure
Inventory sources and permissions → compute comparable measures → classify claims → reject unsupported extensions → draft a bounded story → create proof-slide outline → audit each sentence against the ledger.

## Output contract
1. Claim register: claim, source, calculation, limits, publication status.
2. 300–500 word story or shorter if evidence is thin.
3. One proof-slide outline and one seller email draft.
4. Rejected claims and reasons.
5. Release gaps: naming, quotations, metrics, and final approval.

## Mode-specific rules
Preserve sample size, scope, method, period, mean versus median, and observational limitations. Do not annualize a short pilot without an explicit separate model. Do not invent quotations or customer approval. A draft approval status is not permission to distribute. Use the supplied synthetic fixture as a demonstration only.
