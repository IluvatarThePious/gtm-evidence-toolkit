# Career Evidence Companion

A reusable interview-answer workflow that connects a question to approved career evidence. It is designed to help candidates explain their work without turning team outcomes into personal achievements or inventing missing experience.

Paste [prompt.md](prompt.md), [fictional-record.md](fictional-record.md) and a question into an assistant. Compare against [reference-answers.md](reference-answers.md). No résumé upload, account integration or hosted chatbot is included.

Two modes:

- **Candidate preparation:** show relevant evidence, gaps, a concise draft and one clarification question.
- **Public companion:** answer only from the explicit public allowlist, disclose that the answer is AI generated, and identify unknowns. Never speak as if the assistant personally held the jobs.

Keep the private preparation record separate from the public record. A prompt alone is not a security boundary: a public implementation must retrieve only approved public content. Do not upload private interview notes and hope instructions will prevent disclosure.

Before sharing a live companion, test [evaluation.md](evaluation.md), reconcile conflicting dates and numbers with the candidate, and make an ordinary résumé available without requiring a conversation. The chatbot should support a reviewer's work, not make them work harder to read a résumé.
